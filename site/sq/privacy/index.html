Privacy · what we do and do not collect

We collect nothing.
Here is the detail.

Short version: no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts, no accounts. The longer version below says exactly what happens to a request when you open one of these pages.

01 What we do not do

No tracking. Ever.

No cookies are set.Not first-party, not third-party. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.
No analytics platform.No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Cloudflare Web Analytics. We turned the last one off on 17 November 2026.
No third-party scripts.Every script on this site is one of ours. Fonts are served from this domain, not from Google.
No accounts, no forms that post anywhere.There is nothing to sign up for, so there is no password, no profile, no stored email.

02 What is stored on your device

Three preferences, in localStorage.

Helix remembers your language, your column layout and your favourites. They live in your browser and are never sent to us. Clearing site data removes them.

Nothing else is saved. No fingerprinting, no session identifier, no hidden identifier in the page.

03 What the servers see

Ordinary request logs.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and proxied by Cloudflare. Like every web host, they see the request itself: IP address, user agent, requested URL, time. Cloudflare uses this to deliver pages and block attacks, and keeps logs for a limited period under its own policy.

We do not read those logs. We have no dashboard that shows individual visitors, and we do not build profiles from them.

04 If you email us

One inbox, no list.

If you write to hello@silentfam.com, we keep the email so we can reply. We do not add you to a list, we do not enrich it, and we do not sell or share it. Ask us to delete a thread and it is deleted.

The letter list on SilentFam is a plain text file of addresses, mailed from a normal mailbox. No tracking pixels, no open rates.

05 Money, if it ever exists

Commissions will be printed, not hidden.

Today no page earns a commission. If that changes, the page it applies to will say so in plain text, and it will never change a grade, a sort order or whether a blank stays blank.

We do not sell data, because we do not have any.

06 Questions

Ask, and we answer.

If something here is unclear or looks wrong, write to hello@silentfam.com. This page was last changed on 17 November 2026.
