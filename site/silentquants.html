Why

We want to create prosperity without sacrificing freedom, privacy or peace of mind. Systematic trading, quantitative research and automation can help build real capability — but only with knowledge, disciplined experimentation, realistic expectations and responsible risk management.

Our goal is not to promise financial success. It is to become capable, independent thinkers who can build and operate trustworthy trading systems.

Approach: a strategy factory

Every strategy moves through fixed stations. It advances only when it passes that station's predefined check, and every decision leaves a record.

Rules card → Generate → Filter → Robustness → Portfolio → Position sizing → Forward test → Live → Review

  • Rules decided in advancePass/fail thresholds are set before testing, not after seeing results.
  • Robustness before beliefOut-of-sample, Monte Carlo and cost stress tests come before any capital.
  • Risk sized to the accountPosition sizing is derived from the account's loss limits with a safety margin.
  • Independent reviewNumbers are recomputed by a second reviewer before anything goes live.
